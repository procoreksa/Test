## Production notes

- **Copyright:** use short Netflix clips only when you're commenting on them (fair use is never guaranteed); build most visuals from maps, mock documents, illustrations and stock B-roll.
- **AI visuals:** any realistic AI recreation of real people or events must be disclosed in YouTube Studio ("altered or synthetic content"). Never present an AI image as a real photo of Anna, Rachel or anyone else in the case.
- **Mock documents** must be visibly labelled "Reconstruction" — never show a fake that could pass for a real bank record.
- **Fact-check before recording:** the figures above (€60M claim, $22M loan, $100K, ~$62K Marrakech bill, ~$320K Netflix payment, sentence and release dates) are as widely reported in press coverage of the 2019 trial; confirm each against a primary news source and keep the links in the video description.
- **Voice:** one consistent narrator, quick pacing, a beat of silence before every twist ("And the twist?").
