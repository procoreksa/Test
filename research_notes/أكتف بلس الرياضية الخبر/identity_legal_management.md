Researcher notes. Access date for all sources: 2026-10-10 unless stated otherwise. Labels used: **مؤكد** (confirmed from the source itself), **مرجح** (likely), **غير مؤكد** (unconfirmed or contradictory), **غير متاح** (not publicly available or could not be accessed).

## 1. Identity verification: who is behind activeplus.com.sa, and which similarly named entities are different

### Takeaway
activeplus.com.sa is a WordPress site under the brand "أكتف بلس / Active Plus". It describes itself as a sports and health club for families and children in Al Khobar. Its contact details are 0581244045, 0581050994 and info@activeplus.com.sa, and its social accounts are @Activeplusksa and activeplusksa. The site does not publish any legal name, so the legal entity that owns the domain is **غير متاح** (not available).
