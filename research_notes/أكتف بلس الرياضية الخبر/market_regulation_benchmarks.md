Access date for all sources: 2026-10-10. Labels: مؤكد = confirmed by a primary or multiple consistent sources; مرجح = likely (one reputable secondary source); غير مؤكد = unverified or conflicting; تقديري = analyst estimate or inference.
Method note: these notes come from web search result summaries plus targeted lookups. One direct fetch (globallawexperts.com) failed with a DNS error, so its content is known only through search snippets. Treat the "Cited Findings" as search-summary level unless a primary page is named.

## 1. Market size and indicators

### Takeaway
Saudi Arabia does not publish an official size for the sports-academy segment. The best available proxies are:
- SURJ (PIF) put the sports sector's GDP contribution at about USD 6.9bn in 2023.
- SURJ put the local sports market at about USD 7.2bn in 2023, rising to a projected USD 22.4bn by 2030.
- IMARC put health and fitness clubs at about USD 1.15bn in 2024.

Participation already exceeds the Quality of Life Program's 40% weekly-activity target on GASTAT's 30-minute measure (48.2% in 2021). Children are the weak spot: only 18.7% of 5–17-year-olds met WHO activity guidelines in 2024. The Eastern Province had 5.13m residents in the 2022 census, about 14% of the national population.

- **Sports sector GDP contribution:** about USD 6.9bn in 2023, up from USD 2.4bn in 2016. SURJ Sports Investments (PIF) report, Dec 2024, projects about USD 16.5bn a year by 2030 (about 1.5% of GDP). - **Under-15 share, national:** World Bank figures (not the census) give 24.6% in 2022 and 23.8% in 2024. [مرجح as a proxy] — [Statistics of the World / WB](https://statisticsoftheworld.com/country/saudi-arabia/population-under-15)

### Inferences
- **Children aged 0–14 in the Eastern Province:** applying the national under-15 share (about 24%) gives roughly 1.2m. [تقديري] The province has a large expatriate workforce, which may skew it toward adult males, so the true child share may be lower.
- **Where the demand case is strongest:** the gap between adult activity (58.5%) and child activity (18.7%), together with the Quality of Life Program's mandate, supports a demand narrative centred on kids and girls. Note that the 40% headline target is already met on GASTAT's lighter 30-minute measure. Future policy pressure is therefore likely to shift to youth activity and depth of activity rather than headline participation. [تقديري]
- **Using market figures in the report:** health-club and pilates figures from market-research houses are not academy revenue. Use them only as directional context, never as a total addressable market (TAM) for academies. [تقديري]

### Gaps

- Eastern Province age breakdown (0–4, 5–9, 10–14, 15–19) from the GASTAT census portal was not retrieved.

## 2. Demand trends (kids, schools, compounds, women, camps, private sector, Ministry of Sport initiatives)

### Takeaway
Policy momentum is clearly positive:
- the Quality of Life Program's participation target;
- the Sports for All Federation's community programmes (beach games, youth tournaments);
- private-sector licensing through Nafes;
- women's participation rising, although it still lags men.

No quantified Khobar-specific demand data was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Licensing platform:** the Ministry of Sport launched the "Nafes" (نافس) platform to license private clubs, academies and gyms. It has three licence types: sports club, sports academy, and gym/sports centre. [مؤكد in substance; launch year not confirmed in snippet] — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1472744?amp)
- **SFA community programmes:** the Sports for All Federation runs nationwide events, for example youth tournaments and beach games across cities and beaches, and an Asir waterfront programme with 45,000+ visitors. [مرجح] — [SFA](https://sportsforall.com.sa/saudi-sports-for-all-federation-launches-second-edition-of-youth-tournaments-games-and-beach-games-active-in-cities-and-beaches-across-the-kingdom/); [SFA Asir](https://sportsforall.com.sa/saudi-sports-for-all-federation-attracts-over-45000-visitors-to-its-asir-waterfront-community-program-php/)
- **Female participation gap:** female weekly participation was 38.3% against 54.8% for males (2021). Girls meeting the WHO guideline were 14% against 23.2% for boys (2024). ## 3. Regulation and compliance

### Takeaway
Licensing is moving to the new unified Sports Law:
- published in the Official Gazette on 12 Dec 2025;
- in force from 11 Jun 2026;
- requires existing entities to regularise within one year, i.e. by about Jun 2027, unless extended.

Separately, a joint Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD) and Ministry of Sport Saudization decision applies 15% localisation to 12 sports roles, including coaches, from 18 Nov 2026. This is about five weeks after the access date and directly affects coach staffing. The Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) has been enforceable since Sep 2024 and requires guardian consent for minors.

### Cited Findings
**Sports Law**
- **Status and effective date:** published 12 Dec 2025 and effective 11 Jun 2026 (180 days after publication). It replaces the Basic Regulation for Sports Federations and the Saudi Arabian Olympic Committee. [مؤكد; multiple law firms] — [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/saudi-arabia-introduces-new-sports-law); [Gowling WLG 2026](https://gowlingwlg.com/en/insights-resources/articles/2026/understanding-saudi-arabia-first-unified-sports-law); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/saudi-arabia's-new-sports-law-key-changes-for-the-sector/1729612); [PwC 2026](https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/tax/documents/2026/saudi-sports-law-reinvented.pdf)
- **Licensing pathway:** academies, coaches and agents fall under a unified licensing pathway (Ministry, relevant federation, or designee). Licences are recorded in a national register of sports entities. [مؤكد] — same sources
- **Regularisation deadline:** in-scope entities must regularise within one year of the effective date, extendable by the Minister. [مرجح] — [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/saudi-arabia-introduces-new-sports-law)
- **Implementing regulations:** due within 180 days of publication. I found no confirmation that they have been issued. [غير مؤكد] — [Gowling WLG](https://gowlingwlg.com/en/insights-resources/articles/2026/understanding-saudi-arabia-first-unified-sports-law)
- **Nafes after the law:** Gowling notes that the law does not say whether Nafes will remain the licensing platform. [مرجح] — [Mondaq/Gowling](https://webiis10.mondaq.com/saudiarabia/sport/1780176/a-new-playbook-for-saudi-sport-understanding-the-kingdoms-first-unified-sports-law)
- **Welfare, insurance and inspection (single source):** Global Law Experts (snippet only; fetch failed) says the law mandates athlete-welfare standards (medical care, insurance, youth protection) and inspection powers with sanctions up to suspension or revocation. [غير مؤكد] — [Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/sports-academy-licensing-saudi-arabia/)
  - This is contradicted by Gowling: the law "does not contain explicit safeguarding provisions for young athletes". [Gowling WLG](https://gowlingwlg.com/en/insights-resources/articles/2026/understanding-saudi-arabia-first-unified-sports-law)
- **Coach credentials:** [غير مؤكد] — [Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/sports-academy-licensing-saudi-arabia/)
  - Football academies: the Saudi Arabian Football Federation (SAFF) framework requires AFC- or FIFA-recognised coaching certificates.
  - Support staff such as physiotherapists and strength and conditioning coaches need credentials from the relevant Saudi professional licensing body.

**Saudization**
- **The decision:** a joint HRSD and Ministry of Sport decision requires 15% Saudization of 12 roles in private sports centres and gyms (men's and women's) with 4 or more employees, effective 18 Nov 2026. [مؤكد; multiple sources] — [KPMG Flash Alert 2025-270](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/flash-alert-2025-270.html); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-to-localise-12-key-jobs-in-private-sector-sports-facilities-by-november-2026-1.500358951); [EIG Law](https://eiglaw.com/saudi-arabia-sets-new-15-saudization-requirement-for-private-gyms/)
- **Roles covered:** sports coach, fitness trainer, professional football coach, professional sports coach, water-sports coach, private sports trainer, athletics coach, sports supervisor, ice-sports coach, equestrian coach, and others. Lists vary slightly between outlets. [مرجح] — [People Matters](https://me.peoplemattersglobal.com/news/economy-policy/saudi-hr-ministry-announces-localization-of-12-job-roles-in-sports-industry-47379)
- **Support available:** support from the Human Resources Development Fund (HRDF, "Hadaf") is available. KPMG flags possible redeployment of expatriates. [مرجح] — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/flash-alert-2025-270.html)

**Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)**
- **Timeline and regulator:** in force since 14 Sep 2023, with enforcement from about 14 Sep 2024. The Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) is the regulator. Fines are up to SAR 5m, doubled for repeat violations. [مؤكد] — [Latham & Watkins 2023](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/2023/12/Saudi-Arabias-data-protection-law-enters-into-force)
- **Guardian consent:** required for data subjects lacking full legal capacity, with measures to verify the guardianship. Executive Regulations Art. 13(3) also says guardian consent must not harm the child's interests. [مرجح] — [Bird & Bird](https://childreninthedigitalworld.twobirds.com/home/saudi-arabia)
- **Age threshold (conflicting):** Bird & Bird says the PDPL sets no explicit age and that minors are under 18 under a separate decree. A vendor summary says under 13. [غير مؤكد] — [Bird & Bird](https://childreninthedigitalworld.twobirds.com/home/saudi-arabia); [Usercentrics](https://usercentrics.com/knowledge-hub/saudi-arabia-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl)

**Pool safety**
- **Civil Defence:** has issued public warnings about pool accidents, stressing child supervision, life rings and non-slip pool edges. [مرجح] — [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/?p=975526)
- **Lifeguard certification in practice:** KSA employers require lifeguard certificates from the Saudi Arabian Swimming Federation or the Saudi Life Saving Federation, plus CPR, first aid and AED. These are job-posting requirements, not legal text. [مرجح as market practice; legal mandate غير مؤكد] — [Sabbar job listings](https://sabbar.com/en/jobs/t-full-time-r-lifeguard); [Sabbar Dhahran](https://sabbar.com/en/jobs/c-dhahran-r-lifeguard)

### Inferences
- **Licensing deadline:** Active Plus must hold or obtain a Ministry of Sport academy licence (Nafes or its successor) and be on the national register by about Jun 2027. DD should request the licence certificate and expiry date, and its status under the new law. [تقديري]
- **Saudization:** if Active Plus has 4 or more staff in these roles, 15% Saudization applies from 18 Nov 2026. A largely expatriate coaching team creates near-term cost and continuity risk. Saudi coaches typically cost more, which partly offsets the HRDF support. [تقديري]
- **Hosted venues:** where Active Plus runs at venues it does not own (hotels, compounds, schools), it is unclear whether the venue's municipal (Balady) and Civil Defence licences cover the academy activity. DD should check contracts. [تقديري]

### Gaps
- **Balady municipal licence:** the activity code and the requirements for a sports-academy commercial premises were not retrieved.
- **Civil Defence safety certificate (Salamah):** requirements specific to sports premises or pools were not retrieved.
- **Pool rules:** no official Ministry of Municipal and Rural Affairs and Housing (MOMRAH) or Civil Defence pool-safety regulation text (lifeguard-to-swimmer ratios, depth signage) was found.
- **Child protection:** no specific Ministry of Sport child-safeguarding regulation was found. Note the Global Law Experts vs Gowling conflict above. The general Child Protection System (2014) was not retrieved in this pass.
- **Insurance:** no market source was found for civil-liability or participant accident insurance practice and premiums.
- **VAT:** the standard rate is widely known to be 15% (since 1 Jul 2020), and the VAT registration threshold is SAR 375k. Neither was verified against the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) in this pass, so confirm before citing.
- **Implementing regulations:** whether the Sports Law implementing regulations have been published as of Oct 2026 is unknown.

## 5. Business-model risks

### Takeaway
The main risks are regulatory transition, Saudization cost, venue dependence, safety (especially aquatic), seasonality, and price competition.
- **Sourced:** the regulatory transition (licensing by about Jun 2027) and the new Saudization cost (from Nov 2026).
- **Not sourced:** venue dependence, aquatic safety, seasonality and price competition are structural judgements; no quantitative studies were found.

### Cited Findings
- **Regulatory transition:** existing entities must regularise within one year of 11 Jun 2026, and sanctions include suspension or revocation. [مرجح] — [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/saudi-arabia-introduces-new-sports-law); [Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/sports-academy-licensing-saudi-arabia/)
- **Saudization:** 15% localisation of coach roles from 18 Nov 2026, with "legal penalties" for non-compliance. [مؤكد] — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-to-localise-12-key-jobs-in-private-sector-sports-facilities-by-november-2026-1.500358951)
- **Aquatic safety:** Civil Defence warnings on pool drownings. [مرجح] — [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/?p=975526)
- **Data protection:** PDPL fines of up to SAR 5m for mishandling children's data. [مؤكد] — [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/2023/12/Saudi-Arabias-data-protection-law-enters-into-force)

### Inferences
- **Seasonality:** expect dips in Ramadan, Eid holidays, and summer (Jun–Aug outdoor heat plus family travel), and a peak at school-term starts. Summer camps and indoor or pool programmes partly offset this. Model 2–3 low months a year at about 50–70% of normal enrolment. [تقديري]
- **Venue dependence:** a hosted-venue model means short notice periods, revenue share or rent escalation, and no control over pool maintenance or licensing. This is a key DD item. [تقديري]
- **Coach dependency:** popular coaches take families with them, and the move to Saudization accelerates turnover. [تقديري]
- **Price competition:** low entry barriers for football and martial arts, and competition from free or subsidised SFA and municipal programmes. [تقديري]

### Gaps
- No quantitative seasonality or churn data was found for KSA academies.
- No public incident or drowning statistics were found specifically for the Eastern Province or for commercial pools.
